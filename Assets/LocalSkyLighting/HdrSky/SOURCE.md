# Overcast Soil (Pure Sky)

HDRI by Sergej Majboroda, sky edits by Jarod Guest, from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/overcast_soil_puresky) under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Imported as a specular-filtered cubemap. For this demo the reflection intensity is 0.36, calibrated against the uniform reference with the plate level and sunlight disabled. The photographic sky is a fixed lighting sample.
