# Hammered Copper Dimple

The textures come from the procedural Hammered Copper Dimple material in the [HyPBR atlas](https://hypbr.vercel.app/atlas/mats/hammered_copper_dimple.json) by [wrong-nebula](https://github.com/wrong-nebula/hypbr). Base color and normal were converted from the atlas WebP files to PNG. The atlas ORM map was repacked for URP Lit with metallic in red, ambient occlusion in green, and inverse roughness in alpha.
